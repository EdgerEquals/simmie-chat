Hi all!!

My name is Bonnie and I am the creator of this website. I made Rebella for teenage girls (older are always welcome!) to feel welcome and safe online, to have a platform to express yourself and learn the things you want to, not the things that everyone else wants you to learn at school. I hope that this site helps you realise that the world is waiting for you. And that nobody else can replicate the wonders that you might bring to it.

Be brave. Be different. Be a rebel.

Live it up sis.

About me by Bonnie

Hello!!

My name is Bonnie and I created this website!

I play soccer, and I love camping and swimming at the beach (even though I'm deathly scared of sharks!!). I paint, and I play the viola, the guitar and a range of other instruments.

I'm a proud Aussie girl!! I love learning about nutrition, herbal medicines and botany, and I adore everlasting flowers!

I hope you have a wonderful day. Sending you all love xx
